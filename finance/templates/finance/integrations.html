All three are read from .env. Change them there and restart the app.

Stock and sales

Checkout takes stock out through the ledger. Every sale becomes a dated movement showing which shipment the units came from, and their cost is posted to Cost of Goods Sold. An oversell is recorded with a note rather than failing the customer's checkout.

Revenue is recognised when you issue an invoice, not at checkout. If you never invoice website orders you will see their cost without their revenue — turn on auto-invoicing above, or invoice them by hand.

Backfill older affiliate activity

Commissions approved and payouts made before the finance module existed are not on the ledger. This posts them. Preview first — nothing is written until you apply it, and anything already posted is skipped.
