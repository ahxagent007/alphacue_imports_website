Flat or reducing?

Flat charges interest on the original amount for the whole term and splits it evenly — the way most informal lending is quoted here.
Reducing balance charges interest on what is still owed, so the split shifts from interest to principal as the loan runs down. This is the standard bank EMI, and works out cheaper for the same headline rate.
