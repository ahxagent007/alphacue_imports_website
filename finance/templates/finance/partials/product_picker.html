{% comment %} Shared product picker for line-item tables. Used by both the invoice and purchase forms. To wire a table up: 1. Put `data-product-picker data-search-url="{% url 'finance:product_search' %}"` on the . Add `data-stock-warning="1"` only where selling more than you hold is a mistake — on a purchase you are buying stock in, so warning about low stock there would be noise. 2. Give the product cell `class="combo"` containing the hidden variant field, a `.product-search` text input, a `.combo-clear` button, a `.combo-panel` and a `.line-warn`. 3. Include this partial once, inside {% block extra_js %}. Fields are filled only if the row actually has them, so an invoice row gets its description, SKU and price while a purchase row just gets the product linked — a purchase records what you paid, which has nothing to do with the shelf price. {% endcomment %}
